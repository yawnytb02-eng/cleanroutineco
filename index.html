A few things to know

What am I actually buying?

A digital PDF logbook you can print at home or fill in on a tablet or phone. Nothing physical ships.

How do I write in it if I don’t print it?

Open the PDF in any annotation app, GoodNotes, Notability, and Adobe Acrobat all work, or use your tablet’s built-in markup tool and write directly on the page.

How fast do I get it?

Instantly. Gumroad emails you the download link the moment you check out, no shipping, no waiting.

Can I buy more than one?

Yes. Add any two logbooks to your cart and enter BUNDLE at checkout to save automatically.

Are the sober logbooks a substitute for treatment?

No. They’re a tool to support a routine you’re already building, not medical or clinical care. Please lean on a real professional for that part.

Something’s wrong with my download. Now what?

Reach out to us through Gumroad and we’ll sort it out.
